Privacy & Policy

schedule Last Updated: 12 November 2026

1. About this portal

APML Portal is an independently created, unofficial website intended primarily to help APML employees use practical work tools. The website is publicly accessible, but its tools and information may not be useful or intended for the general public. It is not the official website of APML or any employer, and it is not operated or endorsed by the company unless the company separately confirms that in writing.

The portal is provided as a convenience. Company names, logos, records, contact details and other third-party materials remain the property or responsibility of their respective owners.

2. Information the portal may handle

  • Optional visitor analytics: only after you allow analytics, the portal creates a random browser identifier and session identifier and records page paths, page views, time on page and technical performance measurements.
  • Administrator notifications: after analytics consent, a low-frequency Telegram notice may tell the portal administrator that someone is online, including the page name, broad device/browser category and approximate location supplied by the hosting provider. When you generate a PDF or download a drive/gallery file, a separate operational Telegram notice may include the event type and file/folder name. The file contents are not attached to these notices.
  • Live homepage counters: after you allow analytics, the homepage may maintain a temporary online-presence entry with a randomly generated session ID and timestamp. It is configured for automatic removal when the connection ends. Aggregate quotation and media-download totals are read for display; new usage-counter updates are made only when analytics consent is enabled.
  • Technical information: after analytics consent, the portal stores broad device, operating-system and browser categories, approximate city/region/country supplied by the hosting provider, page paths, time-on-page and performance measurements. The portal analytics database does not store visitor IP addresses, page titles or referrer URLs in new records. A temporary random online-presence marker and timestamp may also be held in Firebase while a consenting visitor is connected; it is configured for automatic removal when the connection ends. Earlier analytics records may still contain legacy fields until those records are cleaned up. Hosting providers may still process IP addresses to deliver and secure the website.
  • Information you submit: a bug report may include the name or designation and description you enter. The report is sent to the portal administrator through Telegram for troubleshooting.
  • Work-tool information: information entered into quotation, directory, rate, drive or gallery features may be processed in the browser or transmitted to supporting services such as Firebase Realtime Database and Cloudinary, depending on the feature you use.

Please do not enter passwords, payment details, personal identification numbers, or confidential/customer information unless you are authorized to use that information in the relevant work process.

3. Service providers and sharing

The portal relies on third-party infrastructure and services, which may process technical or feature-related information to provide hosting, database, file storage, fonts, scripts, notifications and analytics. These include Vercel, Google Firebase, Cloudinary, Telegram, Google Fonts and external content-delivery networks used by particular pages. Their own terms and privacy policies apply to their services.

The About page may link to a separate external support page for optional voluntary contributions. If you follow that link and choose to contribute, payment details are entered on the external page and are handled under the payment provider’s and page operator’s own privacy terms. APML Portal does not ask you to enter or send payment card, bank or UPI credentials through its own pages. Review the external page before submitting payment information.

Information is not sold by the portal for advertising. Information may be disclosed where necessary to operate a feature you request, respond to a report, protect the service, or comply with applicable law.

4. Consent, storage and your choices

Optional visitor analytics and related usage counters are not started unless you select Allow. Selecting Decline saves that choice in this browser and keeps optional analytics disabled. You can revisit your choice from the Cookies & Storage Policy page. You may also email the project maintainer to ask about, correct or request deletion of information you submitted. We may need enough information to verify the request, and some records may remain with third-party providers or where retention is required for security or legal reasons. Essential browser storage and storage required by a feature may still be used to make the website work. See the Cookies & Storage Policy for the current storage-key details.

5. Security and retention

Reasonable technical measures are used within the portal and its service providers, but no website or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure. Data may remain in the relevant service until it is deleted or no longer needed for the feature or operational purpose. A fixed retention period has not been established for every service; do not submit information that should not be retained by these systems.
