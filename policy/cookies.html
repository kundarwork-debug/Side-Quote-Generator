Cookies & Storage Policy

A clear explanation of the small pieces of browser storage APML Portal uses, what optional visitor analytics means, and how you can change your choice.

Last reviewed: 11 November 2026
shield Read privacy policy

1. What do we mean by cookies?

Cookies are small pieces of information a website may store in your browser. APML Portal also uses browser storage such as localStorage and sessionStorage. These technologies can remember preferences or maintain a temporary browser session; they are not all technically cookies.

This page uses the term “cookies & storage” to explain these technologies together.

2. Essential storage

Essential storage supports core portal behaviour and preferences. It remains active so the website can function, regardless of your optional analytics choice.

3. Optional visitor analytics

Visitor analytics is optional and starts only after you choose “Accept all” or save customized preferences with analytics enabled. It helps us understand portal usage and improve reliability and navigation.

When enabled, the portal's analytics script may use the following information:

  • A browser visitor identifier and a session identifier.
  • The page path and page title visited.
  • The referring page address, when the browser provides one.
  • Time spent on a page, reported in short intervals.
  • Technical request information that the analytics service may use to derive an approximate city, masked IP address, or device category.

The portal's consent prompt states that analytics does not use precise location or cross-site tracking. Analytics identifiers are stored in browser storage and are not used to make the portal's core features conditional on consent.

4. Your choices

  • Accept all: enables optional visitor analytics and saves that choice in this browser.
  • Reject optional: keeps essential storage available and does not enable optional analytics.
  • Customize: lets you choose whether optional visitor analytics is enabled.
  • Close the prompt: dismisses it for the current browser session; it may appear again on a later visit.

You can revisit the consent prompt at any time using the “Review my choices” button at the top of this page. Your choice applies to this browser and may need to be made again on another device or browser.

5. Clearing browser storage

You can clear website data through your browser's privacy or site-settings controls. Clearing it may reset saved preferences, remove analytics identifiers, sign you out of services, or affect other portal features. If you clear the consent record, the portal may ask for your analytics choice again.

6. Changes to this policy

This explanation may be updated when the portal's storage or analytics behaviour changes. The review date at the top indicates the latest editorial review of this page.
