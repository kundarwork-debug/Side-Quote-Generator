How to play
  • Move your pawn one square at a time. Reach the opposite edge first to win.
  • On your turn you may either move your pawn or place one wall (you have 10).
  • Walls block movement between squares. They cannot overlap or cross, and you may never block a pawn's path completely.
  • If the pawns face each other you jump over; if a wall is behind them you can step diagonally.
  • To place a wall: press Wall, pick flat or upright with Rotate (or the R key), then click the spot. On a touch screen, tap once to preview and tap again to confirm.
